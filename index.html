About Mama Ada Grand Kitchen & Lounge in Enugu

Mama Ada Grand Kitchen & Lounge is a restaurant in GRA, Enugu State, Nigeria, created to celebrate the rich flavours and culinary traditions of Igbo cuisine.

From Nkwobi, Abacha and Isi Ewu to Egusi soup, Ofe Akwu, Suya and fresh palm wine, our menu brings together familiar Nigerian flavours in a comfortable dining and lounge environment.

Whether you are visiting for a meal, meeting friends, celebrating an occasion or relaxing in the evening, Mama Ada Grand Kitchen & Lounge welcomes you.
